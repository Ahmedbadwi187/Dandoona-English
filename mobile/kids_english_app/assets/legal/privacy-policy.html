Privacy Policy

App: Dandoona English (دندونة إنجليش) · Effective date: [date] · Version: draft 0.1

1. Who we are

The data controller is [owner / company name], [address], contact: [privacy contact email]. The app helps Arabic-speaking children aged 3 to 8 learn English: letters and sounds, then reading and simple sentences, through pictures, audio and touch.

2. The short version

  • The app works fully offline. By default nothing leaves the device.
  • There are no advertisements, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • Children never create accounts and never type personal data. Only a parent can (optionally) create an account to sync progress.
  • The child's voice recording is played back on the device and deleted immediately. It is never uploaded or analysed.
  • A parent can delete a child's data, or the whole account, from inside the app at any time.

5. Microphone

The microphone is used only in the “record and listen” activity, and only after the child taps the record button and the device asks for permission. The recording is a temporary file that is played back once, right after the original word, and then deleted. It is not scored, not sent anywhere and not kept. If permission is denied the activity can be skipped.

6. Children and parental control

The app is designed for children and is directed at parents as account holders. A parental gate (press-and-hold plus a calculation) protects the parent area and settings. The child area has no ads, no purchases and no links that leave the app. Any account or sync is created and controlled by a parent, who gives consent on the child's behalf.

On the device: until the profile is deleted or the app is removed. On the server: until the parent deletes the child or the account. Deleting a child or the account removes the data from our database immediately. [State backup retention, if any.]

9. Your rights and how to delete data

Inside the app a parent can delete a single child (Parent area › Child profiles) or the whole account (Settings › Account & sync › Delete account & data). See the account deletion page. To ask for access to or correction of data, or if you cannot use the app, write to [privacy contact email]. 10. Security

Passwords are stored only as salted hashes; sign-in sessions are short-lived and renewable tokens that are single-use; connections use HTTPS; access to a child's data is limited to the parent who created it; repeated failed sign-ins are rate-limited and temporarily locked.

We will update this page and the effective date when the app's data practices change, and will ask for consent again where the law requires it.
