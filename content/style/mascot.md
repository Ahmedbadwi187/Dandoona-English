# Mascot: Dandoona (دندونة)

Dandoona is the app's main character. She is the owner's own original character from their YouTube channel, not generated
for this app and not based on any third-party character. The locked reference is `mascot.reference.webp`
(transparent 1024 x 1024 cut-out of the channel's character art, copied into this repo; the app does not depend on the
original project folder).

Look: a round, fluffy plum-purple pom-pom body with a lighter lavender belly patch, big dark-blue sparkly eyes, rosy pink
cheeks, a small open smile, short round arms (one waving) and stubby feet. One distinctive feature that is easy to spot at
any size: a glossy yellow music note balanced on top of her head.

Brand colours (from the channel): plum #9B6BD3, sunflower #FFC93C, night ink #2E1F47 (all in `palette.json`).

Rules for any new picture of her:
- Always use the locked reference. Do not redraw her face, change her colours or remove the music note.
- Accessories (hat, glasses, bow, crown, bow tie) are drawn separately as SVG and laid over the reference; they must keep the
  note visible.
- Keep her friendly: always smiling or surprised, never angry or scared.
